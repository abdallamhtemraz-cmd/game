AFTER BUYING YOUR DOMAIN

1. Point the domain to your production frontend host.
2. Set the exact HTTPS domain as the Supabase Auth Site URL.
3. Add the same HTTPS domain to the Supabase Redirect URLs allow-list.
4. Replace YOUR-DOMAIN.com in robots.txt and sitemap.xml.
5. Add https://YOUR-DOMAIN.com to Google Search Console and submit /sitemap.xml.
